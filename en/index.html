Machine Learning · NLPRAM analysis

Natural-language failure classifier

Technicians log failures as free text, which made it hard to know which part was replaced and how often. I trained a natural-language processing model that reads technical notes, detects when a part was changed, replaced or modified, and extracts what failed and what was replaced.

  • Hand-labeled 139 records to train the model.
  • The model segmented 2,992 failures by replaced component.
  • It is the basis of the department's RAM (reliability, availability, maintainability) analysis.

Bank transactions platform

Treasury sent bank statements in Excel and Accounting keyed them in by hand: copy, paste and search for payees in PDFs one by one. This application takes the file, validates it, stores every transaction with its version history and keeps a full audit trail.

  • Sign-in with company accounts (Active Directory).
  • Database migrations with Alembic and automated tests with pytest.

  • Interface tested at six screen widths, from 320 to 1920 px.

Production leftovers calculation

An algorithm that recursively walks the product structure, from basic component to finished good. It multiplies quantities along every parent-child link, derives real consumption across the chain and computes what was left over at each level.

How I work

Method

This is how I take a process from paper or Excel to a system. I documented it as the company's internal automation procedure, in ISO 9000 format.

  1. AS-IS discoveryI interview the people doing the work and map the real flow, the data sources and the rules nobody had written down.
  2. TO-BE designTogether with the team I define the future process: what goes away, what gets validated and who approves.
  3. SpecificationData model, quality rules, permissions and acceptance criteria.
  4. Build and validateI build, test with real data and validate with users before release.
  5. Document and closeManuals, training and KPIs to measure before and after.
